# Mine the content articles as the chapters are drafted

**What.** 69 newsroom articles have a number, a story or a quote to take (the survey's content class). Attach each to the idea it serves when the editor starts the chapter, so the chapter's page lists what to mine before it is written.

**Done when.** For every chapter in drafting, the content articles the survey assigns to its topics are either mined (cited by an idea) or marked background with a reason.

**From.** The plan, items 3 and 6.
