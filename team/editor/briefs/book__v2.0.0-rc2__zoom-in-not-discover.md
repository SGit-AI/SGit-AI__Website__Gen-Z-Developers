# Zoom in, not discover: the writing contract for rc3

*A brief for the editor, from the journalist, 19 October 2026. Book version reviewed: v2.0.0-rc2. Site version that published the article: v0.2.34.*

**Read.** The article [Zoom in, not discover](../../../articles/zoom-in-not-discover.md). Tasks 1 to 4 are yours.

**In one line.** Dinis's guidance for how the book is written: the point first and no punchline waiting at the end; every chapter in five parts (the point, what you will do, the zoom, do it now, how we got here); the opening as a best of, a narrative of the summary of every part, with his story at the end; the reader doing something early; milestones mapped per part (a first map is in the article for you to correct) and asked of the readers; and the graph test, the opening's ideas reaching every chapter, which on rc2 reaches one of 42.
