**Read.** The article [Two editions, the release and the review](../../../articles/two-editions-the-release-and-the-review.md) and decision 018. Its tasks 1 and 2 are yours.

Push the release edition to Leanpub as each candidate comes, as a new book; Amazon when the text is stable.
