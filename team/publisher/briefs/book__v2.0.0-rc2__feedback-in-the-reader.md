# Feedback: a voice memo on a paragraph, through the vault

*A brief for the publisher, from the journalist, 19 October 2026. Site version that published it: v0.2.40.*

**Read.** The section *A better way to get feedback* in [Paper that talks both ways](../../../articles/paper-that-talks-both-ways.md); task 7 is yours.

**In one line.** Reviewing has three moments (consume, capture, package and send) and feedback dies in the third. Propose, for Dinis to decide, a voice memo attached to a paragraph in the reader (`read/`), kept in the browser with the comments and exported into the book's vault with the rest of a review: the reader's rules stand, it sends nothing by itself, and the vault carries it encrypted.
