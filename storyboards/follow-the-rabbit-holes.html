Follow the rabbit holes

Provenance, explainability and asking for the evidence; and being the best-prepared person in the room. 10 panels.

Status: draft · book v1.0.0 is the only official version; the second edition is in release candidates (rc1 frozen, rc2 in progress) · gen-z.journalist, 18 October 2026, from Dinis's memo on questioning and evidence. Every line the Guide says, and every quote card, is from the article 'Follow the rabbit holes', word for word; the build checks it.
