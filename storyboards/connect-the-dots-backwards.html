Connect the dots backwards

Paths, mixing disciplines, learning as a lifelong skill, and why to read this on paper. 14 panels.

Status: draft · book v1.0.0 is the only official version; the second edition is in release candidates (rc1 and rc2 frozen) · gen-z.journalist, 18 October 2026, from Dinis's memo on paths and learning. Every line the Guide says, and every quote card, is Dinis's words from the article 'Connect the dots backwards', word for word, including the Jobs quote as the article gives it; the build checks it.
