Where the thinking came from

The influences behind the book, as claims you can check, and where each one lands. 10 panels.

Status: draft · book v1.0.0 (the second edition does not exist yet) · gen-z.journalist, 18 October 2026, from the review of influences.sgit.ai. Every line the Guide says, and every quote card, is from the article 'Where the thinking came from', word for word; the build checks it. Real people appear as quote cards and dates, never as cast members.
