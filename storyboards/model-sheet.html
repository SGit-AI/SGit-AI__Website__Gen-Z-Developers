The model sheet

Every character in every mood and every pose, side by side, the way animators check a character is on model: a face that reads wrong here reads wrong in every panel it is in. Correct the drawing in assets/storyboard.js or the look in cast.json, rebuild, and every panel changes with it.

Moods: calm, confused, determined, excited, happy, neutral, sad, surprised, thinking, worried. Poses: arms-up, point, shrug, sit, stand, think, wave.
