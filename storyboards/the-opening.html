The opening: you are lucky to be here

The first pages of the second edition as Dinis described them, in 16 panels: wonder, not fear; the history; technology that finally works for you; learning made for you; and why that makes you a developer.

Status: draft · book v1.0.0 is the only official version; the second edition is in release candidates (rc1 and rc2 frozen) · gen-z.journalist, 18 October 2026, from Dinis's memo on the core idea. Every line the Guide says, and every quote card, is Dinis's words from the article 'You are lucky to be here', word for word; the build checks it. The other characters' lines are the storyboard's.
