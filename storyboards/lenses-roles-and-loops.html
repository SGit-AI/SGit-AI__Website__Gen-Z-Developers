Lenses, roles and loops

How LLMs and agents work: a prompt as a lens, a role as a model with a description, an agent as a loop, tools as requests, no memory, compression, and focused threads. 13 panels.

Status: draft · book v1.0.0 is the only official version; the second edition is in release candidates (rc1 and rc2 frozen) · gen-z.journalist, 18 October 2026, from Dinis's memo on LLMs and agents. Every line the Guide says, and every quote card, is from the article 'Lenses, roles and loops', word for word; the build checks it. The five lenses on recursion, the weather question and the named threads are the storyboard's illustrations.
