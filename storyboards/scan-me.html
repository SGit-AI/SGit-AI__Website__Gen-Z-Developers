Scan me

Marta meets a word she does not know on page 12, and a code in the margin. 6 panels.

Status: draft · book v2.0.0-rc2 (a release candidate) · gen-z.journalist, 19 October 2026, from Dinis's third paper review. Every line the Guide says is from the agent article 'RC2 on paper, part 3', word for word, and each is Dinis's note from the printed page; the build checks it.
