The 2018 book, as a storyboard

Book v1.0.0, the 28 published chapters, converted into 15 panels: what the first edition said, who it said it to, and where it stops.

Status: draft · book v1.0.0 · gen-z.journalist, 18 October 2026, a first conversion for Dinis to review. Every line the Guide says, and every quote card, is the 2018 text word for word, with its chapter as the source, unless the panel's note says otherwise. Lines the other characters say are the storyboard's, and say what the reader is thinking. Swipe, use the arrow keys, or the buttons; Notes shows what each panel is for and its source; Full screen for an iPad.

The data is the-2018-book.json and cast.json; the panels are drawn in your browser from it, by assets/storyboard.js with Rough.js. Nothing is an image file: change the JSON, rebuild, and every panel is redrawn.
