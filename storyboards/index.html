Storyboards

The book as a sequence of panels: the punchline, the concepts, the evidence and the people they are for, one panel at a time, to flick through on a tablet before a word of a chapter is written. A storyboard is data and the panels are drawn from it by code, so a panel is corrected by changing a line, not by redrawing a picture.

The storyboards

  1. The 2018 book, as a storyboard: Book v1.0.0, the 28 published chapters, converted into 15 panels: what the first edition said, who it said it to, and where it stops. (15 panels, draft)
  2. A first storyline for the second edition: A proposal in 15 panels: the order the second edition could tell its argument in, built from the topics the newsroom's articles already cover. For Dinis to tear apart. (15 panels, proposal)

The cast

The cast of the Generation Z Developers storyboards, v0.1, proposed by gen-z.journalist on 18 October 2026 for Dinis to accept, change or replace. Characters are drawn programmatically by assets/storyboard.js from 'look'; nothing here is an image. Fictional people: each stands for a reader the book is for (the target audience), or for a role in the story. Names chosen to avoid clashing with the riskmandate.ai cast (which has a Maya) and with the word 'developer' (no 'Dev').

How it works

  • The data. admin/content/storyboards/cast.json, and one NN__<slug>.json per storyboard: scenes of kind cover, scene, evidence (a quote card or a bar chart), punchline or spectrum; each with a caption, the people in it (who, where, pose, mood, what they say or think), props, a note on what the panel is for, and its source.
  • The drawing. assets/storyboard.js draws people, bubbles, captions, props and charts as SVG with Rough.js (MIT, vendored in assets/vendor/), so everything looks sketched; a fixed seed per panel keeps the wobble the same on every load. In the deck the strokes draw themselves and the people and bubbles arrive in order.
  • The checks. The build refuses a scene whose character is not in the cast, an unknown pose, mood or kind, or an evidence panel with nothing in it.
  • No generated images. Nothing here is drawn by an image model; that keeps every panel correctable, versioned and free of licence questions. Image models can still be given a storyboard as a brief, the way riskmandate.ai's stories do.
