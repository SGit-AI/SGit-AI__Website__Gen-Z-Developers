Everything is connected

What a graph is (not a diagram), the whodunit, a graph becoming a map, groups of groups, provenance, and starting anywhere. 12 panels.

Status: draft · book v1.0.0 is the only official version; the second edition is in release candidates (rc1 and rc2 frozen) · gen-z.journalist, 18 October 2026, from Dinis's memo on graphs. Every line the Guide says, and every quote card, is from the article 'Everything is connected', word for word; the build checks it. The whodunit, the forest and the provenance chain are the storyboard's illustrations.
