Where do you land?

Wardley maps, shown before they are named, and the three centres of gravity: explorer, villager, town planner. 14 panels.

Status: draft · book v1.0.0 (the second edition does not exist yet) · gen-z.journalist, 18 October 2026, from Dinis's memo on Wardley maps. Every line the Guide says, and every quote card, is Dinis's words from the article 'Where do you land?', word for word; the build checks it. The map of Zara's first job is the storyboard's illustration, not Dinis's. Swipe, use the arrow keys, or the buttons; Notes shows what each panel is for and its source; Full screen for an iPad.
