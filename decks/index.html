How a deck is made

  • The data. admin/content/decks/<slug>/deck.json (the title, the order, the fonts) and one slides/<id>.htm per slide (a fragment, not a page of this site): one <section> on a 1920 by 1080 canvas, every style inline, the speaker notes in its <aside>. Images are named by their path here: the covers, the infographics, a storyboard panel.
  • The drawing. python3 admin/build/deck_pdf.py, after the site build, renders the PDF and the slide images with Chromium; both are committed, as the book's PDF is. The build checks every slide, image and page.
  • The same files are in the subset the Slides artifact type reads, so a deck can be published there for comments and presenting, and back.
