Generation ZX Developers (GenAI Startup Edition) v2.0.0-rc2

What this is. Release candidate v2.0.0-rc2 of the second edition: 41 chapters written from the ideas register, for Dinis to read and mark up. Not an official version. Keys: j/k next and previous chapter, g the graph, f focus.

Review mode. Each chapter has a state: unread, reading, read, to review, continue, signed off. Comment on any paragraph. Export the review as markdown or JSON for the vault's reviews/ folder. Nothing here is sent anywhere unless you export it; this reader keeps its own store (genz.reader.v2), apart from the 2018 book's.

The graph. The open chapter in the middle, its part and neighbours, and the articles it was written from; the ideas it carries are listed under it, each a link to the register.
