The versions of the book

The rule this site is built on: the repository holds the official versions; the vault holds the working versions. A version moves from the vault into the repository when it is signed off, on top of the one before, and this page shows the delta between every two versions, block by block and word by word. There is one version so far.

VersionDateReleaseWordsChange from the version before
v1.0.02018-09-30v0.2.031,097first publishedthe text

The table and the deltas are computed by the newsroom's page_versions.py (newsroom v0.12.113), copied into admin/build/ as the brief asked rather than rewritten. Each version is frozen as one markdown file by the release that made it and never rewritten; the frozen file for v1.0.0 is v1.0.0.md, 31,097 words in 1,106 blocks: the book as published, the 28 chapters of the print PDF (139 pages, sha256 6accc25c0c8b…) and the Leanpub build. The drafts the build left out are not part of any version; they are kept as v1/book/drafts.md and shown on their own page.

Release candidates of the second edition

A candidate is a draft of the next edition written in this repository for Dinis to read and mark up (decision 009). It is frozen once, like a version, so that what was read cannot change; it is not official and nothing is published from it outside this site.

  • v2.0.0-rc1, 2026-10-19, Gen Z Developers (GenAI Edition): The first end-to-end draft of the second edition, written by the book's agents from the ideas register (106 ideas, 50 of them Dinis's own positions from his co-written articles) and the articles, decisions and 2018 text behind them, in the proposed chapter map's order; the hands-on part, dated 2026, is included so that Dinis can decide whether it stays inside the book or becomes a companion. Where only Dinis can supply something, the chapter says so in a note. Frozen at v2/versions/v2.0.0-rc1.md, sha256 0d8f6e1de8e2…
  • v2.0.0-rc2, 2026-10-19, Generation ZX Developers (GenAI Startup Edition): The book rewritten under the Gen ZX frame Dinis chose (decision 014): two generations, the founder as the reader, the objective that by the end the reader has started a company, a new part Start the company at the centre, Building, safely rebuilt as Build it without a server, a new first chapter and four more hands-on chapters, the measure in the last chapter; the round-1 batch of newsroom articles processed into ideas and written in. Where the newsroom has no article yet (the UK company, Lithuania, Portugal, the EU, the first customers, the community), the chapter stands as its title, its ideas and what is missing. Frozen at v2/versions/v2.0.0-rc2.md, sha256 5ed445042a0b…

Where v1.0.0 comes from

  • The repository DinisCruz/Book_Generation_Z_Developer at commit 61886444fe5a (1 March 2024, a README change; the content was last changed in October 2018). Its content/ folder is copied verbatim to v1/content/ and its static/images/ to v1/images/: 138 markdown files, of which the Hugo layouts published the 28 marked status: content; 90 are outlines and the rest drafts.
  • The print-ready PDF, v1/generation-z-print-ready.pdf: 139 pages, the Leanpub build of 8 October 2018 made print-ready for the paperback, supplied by Dinis on 18 October 2026. Its table of contents is the published book's, and is kept as data in admin/content/book/print-toc.json.
  • Leanpub: "Key concepts and ideas for the next generation of developers", 60% complete, 136 pages, 30,695 words, last updated 8 October 2018, 465 readers (read 18 October 2026). The Leanpub build left the drafts and outlines out, as the print edition did; this site shows them apart from the book, because the second edition starts from them.
  • Amazon: Gen Z Developers, the paperback: 139 pages, published 30 September 2018, ISBN-10 1724194607, the early-release cover of September 2018 with its eight lines. That publication date is the date of v1.0.0; 8 October 2018 is the last change to the text, on Leanpub and in the repository.
