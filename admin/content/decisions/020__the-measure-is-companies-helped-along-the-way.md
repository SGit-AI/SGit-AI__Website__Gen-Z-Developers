# The measure is the companies helped along the way

**Decided.** 2026-10-19

**By.** Dinis Cruz.

**Said.** "When we say how many companies we helped to create, I think that's too much. I think we should say how many companies we helped along the way. Some got created, some didn't got created, right? I think that's more, it also sounds less arrogant."

**Means.** The book measures itself by the companies it helped along the way, created or not, not by the companies it created. A founder who shipped, found out nobody missed it and stopped, and learned from it, counts. The first chapter, the last chapter and the website say it this way, and the community collects the stories, including the ones that did not become companies.

**Supersedes.** The measure as written in [Gen ZX Developers](/articles/gen-zx-developers.md) ("how many companies have been created from it").

**Recorded in.** [Generation ZX Founders](/articles/generation-zx-founders.md).
