# The title is Generation ZX Developers (GenAI Startup Edition)

**Decided.** 2026-10-19

**By.** Dinis Cruz.

**Said.** "Ok agreed, let's go with "Generation ZX Developers (GenAI Startup Edition)"."

**Means.** The second edition's title is *Generation ZX Developers (GenAI Startup Edition)*. It replaces the title of decision 010, *Gen Z Developers (GenAI Edition)*, and accepts the title part of the proposal in [Gen ZX Developers](/articles/gen-zx-developers.md): the book is for Generation Z and Generation X, and its objective is a startup by the end of it. The singular is deliberate: *Generation ZX* is one name made of two generations, one letter away from the 2018 *Generation Z Developers*, and the two generations are said in full on the cover line and in the opening. "Gen ZX" is the short form; never "XZ". "GenAI" is written as one word everywhere. rc1 is frozen and keeps the old title; rc2 carries this one. Still open: whether this is the second edition of *Generation Z Developers* or a new book, and with it Leanpub, the ISBN, Amazon and this site's address (the publisher's brief); and the rest of the Gen ZX proposal (the audience, the objective, the new part, the remapping), which goes to the board as decided in 008.

**Supersedes.** The title in [decision 010](/decisions/index.html#the-title-is-gen-z-developers-genai-edition); decision 010 stays on the record.

**Recorded in.** [Gen ZX Developers](/articles/gen-zx-developers.md).
