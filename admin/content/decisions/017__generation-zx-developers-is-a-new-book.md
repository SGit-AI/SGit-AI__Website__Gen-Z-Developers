# Generation ZX Developers is a new book

**Decided.** 2026-10-19

**By.** Dinis Cruz, in pen, on page 9 of the printed release candidate 2.

**Said.** Beside the question "is this the second edition of *Generation Z Developers*, or a new book that grew out of it?", with "new book that" circled: "new book".

**Means.** *Generation ZX Developers (GenAI Startup Edition)* is a new book that grew out of *Generation Z Developers* (2018), not its second edition. The book stops calling itself an edition, and says less about the 2018 book: on page 8 Dinis wrote "this is going to be a big rewrite", and the 2018 story moves to the section at the end on how the book was written and evolved. How it reaches Leanpub's 465 readers is the publisher's question in [Version 2 on Leanpub](/articles/version-2-on-leanpub.md), where Dinis's starting point was to update the 2018 listing; that, and the ISBN, follow from this decision and are the publisher's to lay out.

**Supersedes.** The open question left by [decision 014](/decisions/index.html#the-title-is-generation-zx-developers-genai-startup-edition).

**Recorded in.** [RC2 on paper, part 2](/agent-articles/rc2-on-paper-pages-4-to-9.md).
