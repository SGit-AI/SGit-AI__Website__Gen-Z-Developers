# A release candidate is a release: shipped on Leanpub and sold, now

**Decided.** 2026-10-19

**By.** Dinis Cruz, in pen, on page 4 of the printed release candidate 2.

**Said.** Beside "This is a candidate, not a version": "WRONG". Beside the paragraph about the candidate: "this is an RC which will be added to Leanpub & sold ... i.e. we are selling & shipping this book now". At the foot of the page: "this RCx → RELEASE CANDIDATE ... i.e. this should be a release → not a draft".

**Means.** A release candidate is released: it is published on Leanpub and sold, now, and it reads as a book, not as a working document. The book does not call itself a draft or "not a version"; it spells out "release candidate". This replaces the part of decision 009 that said a candidate is "not a version" and that "nothing is published from it outside this site": the candidate is still frozen once and read on this site from that snapshot, and its working copy is still the vault's; what changes is that the frozen candidate in this repository is what the publisher uploads. The rule that nothing is published from the vault stands. It answers the question kept apart in [Version 2 on Leanpub](/articles/version-2-on-leanpub.md) ("upload at v2.0.0, or now?"): now.

**Supersedes.** In [decision 009](/decisions/index.html#a-candidate-is-read-on-the-site-from-a-frozen-snapshot), "not a version" and "nothing is published from it outside this site"; the rest of decision 009 stands.
