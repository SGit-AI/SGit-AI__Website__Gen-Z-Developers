# The title is Gen ZX Developers, the GenAI Startup Edition, for two generations, with one objective

**Decided.** 2026-10-19

**By.** Dinis Cruz.

**Said.** "I think we're already ready for RC2, because if you pull from the repo, you'll find the new positioning, which I think is very exciting [...] can you basically recreate the book taking that into account, and the new title and the new positioning and the content that we want to add? [...] now we have two generations and we really focus now on that whole idea of creating a company, right? The whole idea of creating a Gen AI company, a startup, and that's my target audience."

**Means.** The proposal in [Gen ZX Developers](/articles/gen-zx-developers.md) is adopted as the frame of release candidate 2, by Dinis's instruction to the librarian to rewrite the book under it. The title is *Gen ZX Developers*; the tagline is *the GenAI Startup Edition*; it is the second edition of the 2018 book, grown out of it. The book is written first for Generation Z, entering the workforce and the new entrepreneurs, and Generation X, the subject-matter experts starting companies, often their parents; the millennials in the middle may read it, and the book says why it is not for them first. The reader is the founder, and the objective is that by the end of the book the reader has started a company; the book is measured by the companies created from it. The chapter map changes as the article maps it: a new part, *Start the company*, at the centre; *Building, safely* rebuilt as *Build it without a server*; a new first chapter; four more hands-on chapters; the measure in the last chapter. The newsroom articles the article lists are in scope and processed into ideas as the round-1 batch. The board (decision 008) hears what comes after rc2; this change set is Dinis's own and did not wait for a hearing.

**Supersedes.** [Decision 010](/decisions/index.html#the-title-is-gen-z-developers-genai-edition), the title *Gen Z Developers (GenAI Edition)*, which stood for one release candidate.

**Recorded in.** [The release candidate, written from the register](/agent-articles/the-release-candidate-written-from-the-register.md), in its rc2 section.
