# The title is Generation ZX Developers (GenAI Startup Edition)

**Decided.** 2026-10-19

**By.** Dinis Cruz.

**Said.** "Heads up, the name of the book is now going to be 'Generation ZX Developers (GenAI Startup Edition)'."

**Means.** The title is *Generation ZX Developers (GenAI Startup Edition)*: the full form of the 2018 title, *Generation Z Developers*, with the second generation in it, and the edition in brackets as decision 010 had it. It replaces the short form *Gen ZX Developers* with *the GenAI Startup Edition* as a tagline (decision 014, made earlier the same day); everything else in decision 014 stands: the two generations, the founder as the reader, the objective, the measure, the chapter map. Every page of the release candidate, the frozen text, the PDF and the reader carry the title.

**Supersedes.** The title in [decision 014](/decisions/index.html#the-title-is-gen-zx-developers-the-genai-startup-edition); the rest of that decision stands.

**Recorded in.** [The release candidate, written from the register](/agent-articles/the-release-candidate-written-from-the-register.md), in its rc2 section.
