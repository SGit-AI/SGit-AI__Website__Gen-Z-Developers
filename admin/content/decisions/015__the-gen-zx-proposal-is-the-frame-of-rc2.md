# The Gen ZX proposal is the frame of release candidate 2

**Decided.** 2026-10-19

**By.** Dinis Cruz.

**Said.** "I think we're already ready for RC2, because if you pull from the repo, you'll find the new positioning, which I think is very exciting [...] can you basically recreate the book taking that into account, and the new title and the new positioning and the content that we want to add? [...] now we have two generations and we really focus now on that whole idea of creating a company, right? The whole idea of creating a Gen AI company, a startup, and that's my target audience."

**Means.** The rest of the proposal in [Gen ZX Developers](/articles/gen-zx-developers.md), beyond the title decision 014 records, is adopted as the frame of release candidate 2 by Dinis's instruction to the librarian to rewrite the book under it; this change set is his own and did not wait for a hearing, so the board (decision 008) hears what comes after rc2. The book is written first for Generation Z, entering the workforce and the new entrepreneurs, and Generation X, the subject-matter experts starting companies, often their parents; the millennials in the middle may read it, and the book says briefly why it is not for them first. The reader is the founder, and the objective is that by the end of the book the reader has started a company; the book is measured by the companies created from it. The chapter map changes as the article maps it: a new part, *Start the company*, at the centre; *Building, safely* rebuilt as *Build it without a server*; a new first chapter; four more hands-on chapters; the measure in the last chapter. The newsroom articles the article lists are in scope and processed into ideas as the round-1 batch. The rc1 map is kept as `chapters-rc1.json` so the rounds page can compare the two.

**Supersedes.** Nothing; it completes [decision 014](/decisions/index.html#the-title-is-generation-zx-developers-genai-startup-edition), which took the title and left the rest to the board.

**Recorded in.** [The release candidate, written from the register](/agent-articles/the-release-candidate-written-from-the-register.md), in its rc2 section.
