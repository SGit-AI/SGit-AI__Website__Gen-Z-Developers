# The title is Generation ZX Founders (GenAI Edition)

**Decided.** 2026-10-19

**By.** Dinis Cruz.

**Said.** "I like Generation ZX founders, Gen AI edition." And, in [You are a developer](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/you-are-a-developer.html): "it is very hard to tell someone what they are in a word they do not relate to, so my hypothesis is that it should be called Generation ZX Founders, with "you are a developer" kept for the end, as the reveal."

**Means.** The book's title is *Generation ZX Founders (GenAI Edition)*. The reader is named by the word they use for themselves, founder; "you are a developer" is the turn of the book, made in the frame chapter (*Everyone who can brief an agent is a developer*), not held back as a twist on the last page, because the book zooms in and keeps no punchline waiting ([Zoom in, not discover](/articles/zoom-in-not-discover.html)). "Startup" leaves the tagline because "Founders" says it; "GenAI" stays, because it dates the book to this revolution. Generation ZX is a mindset more than a range of birth years: the people building because finally they can, seen most in Generation Z and Generation X. Short form: Gen ZX Founders; never "XZ"; "GenAI" as one word.

**Supersedes.** The title in [decision 014](/decisions/index.html#the-title-is-generation-zx-developers-genai-startup-edition); decisions 014 and 010 stay on the record.

**Recorded in.** [Generation ZX Founders](/articles/generation-zx-founders.md).
