What this site does not claim

  • The second edition is not written. This release has the first edition, the map, the roles, the vault and the versions machinery. The review of the 2018 text and the editor's interviews come next.
  • The map is a first reading. Not every outline has an article, and not every article fits; the reviewer and the editor decide.
  • The address is the one proposed. gen-z-developers.sgit.ai, matching the printed cover's Gen Z Developers; to confirm.
  • The licence of the 2018 text is to confirm with Dinis: CC BY on the cover, CC BY-SA in the footer, Apache 2.0 on the repository.
