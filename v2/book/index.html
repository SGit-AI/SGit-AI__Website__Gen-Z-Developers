Generation ZX Developers (GenAI Startup Edition)

Release candidate v2.0.0-rc2, 2026-10-19: 41 chapters in 10 parts, 73,314 words, written from 198 of the 198 ideas in the register, with 47 places where only Dinis can fill in what is missing. The book rewritten under the Gen ZX frame Dinis chose (decision 014): two generations, the founder as the reader, the objective that by the end the reader has started a company, a new part Start the company at the centre, Building, safely rebuilt as Build it without a server, a new first chapter and four more hands-on chapters, the measure in the last chapter; the round-1 batch of newsroom articles processed into ideas and written in. Where the newsroom has no article yet (the UK company, Lithuania, Portugal, the EU, the first customers, the community), the chapter stands as its title, its ideas and what is missing.

A release candidate, not a version. This is v2.0.0-rc2 of the second edition: every chapter written by the book's agents from the ideas register, for Dinis to read, print and mark up. Nothing in it is signed off. The first edition, v1.0.0, is the only official version; the candidate's text is frozen at v2/versions/v2.0.0-rc2.md so that what was read cannot change behind the reader (decision 009).

How to read it, and how to answer

Read it as a reader first: the reader keeps your place, lets you mark each chapter (read, to review, continue, signed off), comment on any paragraph, and export the review as a file for the vault's reviews/ folder. Or print the one-page version or the PDF and write on it. Every chapter ends with where it came from: the ideas, the articles and the 2018 files, each a link. A verb on an idea (keep, rewrite, merge, cut, article first, later, not doing it) is a decision; the editor records it in the vault and the idea's state moves on the register.
