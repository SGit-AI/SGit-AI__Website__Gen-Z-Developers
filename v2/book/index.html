A release candidate, not a version. This is v2.0.0-rc1 of the second edition: every chapter written by the book's agents from the ideas register, for Dinis to read, print and mark up. How to read it, and how to answer

Read it as a reader first: the reader keeps your place, lets you mark each chapter (read, to review, continue, signed off), comment on any paragraph, and export the review as a file for the vault's reviews/ folder. Or print the one-page version or the PDF and write on it. Every chapter ends with where it came from: the ideas, the articles and the 2018 files, each a link. A verb on an idea (keep, rewrite, merge, cut, article first, later, not doing it) is a decision; the editor records it in the vault and the idea's state moves on the register.
