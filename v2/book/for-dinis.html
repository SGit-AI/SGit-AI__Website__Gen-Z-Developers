Everything that waits on Dinis

The release candidate was written from the register, and the register was written from the articles; where the articles stop, the chapters stop and say so. This page collects every For Dinis note in the 41 chapters, and the ideas the map has not placed.

A release candidate, not a version. This is v2.0.0-rc2 of the second edition: every chapter written by the book's agents from the ideas register, for Dinis to read, print and mark up. Nothing in it is signed off. The first edition, v1.0.0, is the only official version; the candidate's text is frozen at v2/versions/v2.0.0-rc2.md so that what was read cannot change behind the reader (decision 009).
