The book as a graph

Everything this site does to make the second edition, as one graph with a state on every node: the sources (articles, decisions, memos, the 2018 book), the ideas they carry, the chapters the ideas go to, the tasks the articles ask for, and the decisions that move them. This is the admin area of the publishing process, and the record of what has been processed, what is placed, and what waits on Dinis. The plan says how it runs.

What waits on Dinis

  • 55 candidate ideas to read, comment on and decide, the read, comment, decide way; the loop's first batch is the editor's task. The register, filtered to candidates.
  • The chapter map: 30 chapters proposed; group by centre of gravity or only mark it; accept the cast; where the storyline starts.
  • The open questions the articles keep apart: the podcast to credit; three jobs that never scaled; the dot-com crash in the opening or later; his own three dots; a printable PDF for pen and paper; the title and the language.

What waits on the roles

  • The librarian: no bring article left to process; the 37 content articles to mine as the chapters are drafted; a pass over the 23 2018 files with neither an article nor an idea.
  • The editor: the chapter map (this one is a proposal), the vault's decisions folder, the first interview, the first batch of the loop.
  • The publisher: release v1.0.0 end to end, the ten tasks.
  • The journalist: the gap articles, the fear and the job hunt first; the evidence the opening needs.

The data: admin/content/graph/ (ideas.json, chapters.json, sources-state.json, tasks-state.json) and graph.json, derived on every build by admin/build/graph_model.py. The build refuses an idea that cites a source that does not exist, a chapter nothing names, or a source marked processed that no idea cites.
