A service only scales with people: a curated marketplace of consultants, joined by proof of work

Agentic engagements need someone who knows the client's world and can steer a hive in it, and there are more of those people in a community than hours in a founder's week. So alongside the offers, a small curated list of consultants who deliver on the same stack, where the client picks the kind of project first and then the people. Joining copies the markets that work: apply, show proof of work (a public vault or repository of a real run with its ledger), peer review by two members, a thirty to forty-five minute interview with a scenario, a first engagement shadowed, and standards including a behaviour policy for consultants, the same idea applied to agents. The consultant sets the price within the band and keeps most of it; markets sit between 5% and 20% in total (Malt charges freelancers 5%, Upwork 0% to 15%, Braintrust 15% to the client).

Decided by. Nobody yet: a candidate for Dinis to read, comment on and decide (keep, rewrite, merge, cut, article first, later, not doing it).

Note. Nothing is built; the platform share is a proposal; the first two consultants (Mondweep Chakravorty and Dinis) are not taking bookings. Toptal says fewer than 3% of applicants pass its five stages. Also a door for the reader going into a job: join as the person who delivers.
