Explorer coding: what people call vibe coding, done as exploring

Anyone with a need explores it by building, with the fast feedback loop that made spreadsheets win; it is not a way to ship, it is a way to find out.

Decided by. Nobody yet: a candidate for Dinis to read, comment on and decide (keep, rewrite, merge, cut, article first, later, not doing it).

Note. What vibe coding describes is people writing very good briefs, which product owners and users who knew what they wanted always did, in a loop of weeks or months on somebody else's terms. The brief was fine; the execution was the bottleneck. Now the loop is minutes, and very few products let you strip out the 90% you do not want and keep the 10% you do, which is exactly what you can now build. The word's history: Andrej Karpathy's post of February 2025 ('forget that the code even exists'), describing throwaway weekend projects; Collins Dictionary's word of the year by November 2025; Andrew Ng in May 2025 calling it 'unfortunate' and coding with AI 'a deeply intellectual exercise'. Exploratory programming is the research ancestor; Dinis found no established use of 'explorer coding'. The HR conversation ('Amazing! When can I start?') is one conversation, an illustration and not a survey. Spreadsheets won because of the feedback loop, a REPL for people who would never call it that.
