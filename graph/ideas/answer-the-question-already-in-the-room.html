Do not sell the need; answer a question somebody already has to answer, with a deadline

There are two groups, the people who see how fast agents are moving and the much larger group who do not, and the second group will not ask for an agent risk tool because they do not see the risk. The way in is a question somebody already has, with a deadline, in the language of the room: a contract, where legal and procurement ask what a vendor's agent will do with the data; a security questionnaire, where a startup's first serious enterprise customer asks which agents touch their data and how they are bounded; an insurance renewal, where the question is whether AI is excluded from cover; the board's risk appetite; and twenty minutes with the assistant you already run. None of these asks anyone to understand agents first.

Decided by. Nobody yet: a candidate for Dinis to read, comment on and decide (keep, rewrite, merge, cut, article first, later, not doing it).

Note. A founder is on both sides: as the seller, and as the startup that, in the article's words, will be diligenced on its agents before long. The insurance argument is RiskMandate's, summarised from its pages, not independently surveyed.
