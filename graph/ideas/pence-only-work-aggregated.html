Below about five pounds the card fee is the price, so pence payments only work aggregated

Stripe's standard UK rate is 1.5% plus 20p: on a £20 payment that is 2.5%, on £5 it is 5.5%, on 50p it is 41.5%, and on 10p it is more than the payment. So payments of pence only work aggregated: one charge a year that covers many projects, or a platform that already holds a balance and settles in bulk, which is how the newsroom's reading meter prices a page at a few pence against £5 of credit settled as one balance. Payment also has to be tied to a verified user who receives something, not to a count: when tea.xyz rewarded package popularity with tokens, one developer published nearly 14,000 packages.

Decided by. Nobody yet: a candidate for Dinis to read, comment on and decide (keep, rewrite, merge, cut, article first, later, not doing it).

Note. The article finds the card-versus-platform difference smaller than expected at £2 to £3, where the 20p fee is under 10%; aggregation matters below about £1.
