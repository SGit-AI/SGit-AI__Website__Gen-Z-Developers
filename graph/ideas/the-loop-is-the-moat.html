The speed of the loop from a comment to a feature is the moat, not the technology

What is defensible is not the technology but the data, the relationships, the distribution, or the speed at which you ship. The loop (a user comments, an article is written, a feature ships, the founder uses it) is what is hard to copy: a reading list made for one person went from brief to live in 81 minutes, a link to a persona in 17 hours. Usage is also what gives a product its direction when agents can build in any direction.

Decided by. Nobody yet: a candidate for Dinis to read, comment on and decide (keep, rewrite, merge, cut, article first, later, not doing it).

Note. The times are from the git logs of sgit.ai and the newsroom, as the article says. a-company-in-the-review already says 'what is defensible is the record and the loop' for one company; this is the general claim for the chapter.
