Be the best-prepared person in the room: briefings, pre-briefings, follow-ups

There is no excuse now not to be the most prepared person in the room: a briefing for you, a briefing on the people you meet, a follow-up after; what used to be overhead for a rare kind of person now scales, like publishing and communication.
