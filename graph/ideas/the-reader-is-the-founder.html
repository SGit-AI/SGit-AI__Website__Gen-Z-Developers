The reader this book is for is the founder, and its objective is that by the end you have started a company

Not founder as an attitude, as the 2018 Be a founder meant it, but literally: in 2026 you can be the founder. The book is the one you give someone who wants to start a GenAI company, and it says how to actually do it, for real: the company, the cloud, the programmes, the first sale.

Decided by. Dinis, in Gen ZX Developers, adopted as the frame of rc2 (decision 015)
