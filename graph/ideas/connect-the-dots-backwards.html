A path only makes sense looking back

Trust your gut going forward and use a method for walking: doors, fast iteration, constraints, leverage. Jobs's calligraphy class became the Mac's typography.

Decided by. Dinis, in Connect the dots backwards; his own three dots are still his to name

Note. Taking SG/Send to market end to end is where Dinis learnt Stripe, pricing and everything else a real product needs, so that by the newsroom he already knew how; 'a good example of how exploring something early pays off later, in a way nobody plans'. And the same technology that did not land as a file-sending service is used now because the newsroom, RiskMandate and the vaults need it: explorers find things early; the use case decides when they matter.
