Gen ZX are developers: coding with agents the explorer coding way, which is what vibe coding should be called

The entrepreneurs the book is for are developers: they code with agents. What people call vibe coding is exploration, and the word should say so; the explorer builds to find out, and what the exploration produces is the brief for the version other people rely on. A founder needs both halves: the exploring and the handover.

Decided by. Dinis, in Gen ZX Developers, adopted as the frame of rc2 (decision 015)
