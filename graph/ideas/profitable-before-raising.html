Fund it, or do not: the worst time to raise is before you are profitable, and the best is when they call you

Raising before the product, before the fit and before profit means bargaining from weakness with a clock running, and the worst terms are not about money but control: a say in the business handed to people whose job is a return, not the mission. Profitability means time, and being able to wait is most of a negotiating position. Do not get signed before you have the album. With agents making execution cheap, getting to that position before you need anyone's money is more possible than it has ever been; then the choice of investor is yours. Not never raise: raise from strength, on terms you set, from people who came to you because the thing already works.

Decided by. Nobody yet: a candidate for Dinis to read, comment on and decide (keep, rewrite, merge, cut, article first, later, not doing it).

Note. The chapter's centre, which the register lacked as its own node. The article's caveat on price: it might not be profitable today and still be right if the economics improve with scale, but keep the gap small enough to self-fund.
