The book is 200 printed pages or more; short and punchy stays the rule for chapters, not for the book

The 2018 paperback was 139 pages and, Dinis says, quite thin; the target is 200 pages or more. The cutter still argues against every addition and the default is still no, but its budget is a book of 200 pages and more: the question for a proposal is whether it earns its place.
