There is a company in this: a code review that reads every layer, for somebody to build

Say the business plainly: a review attached to a repository that keeps the layered graph current per commit and shows each change at every altitude, sold to anyone generating code faster than they can review it, and to teams whose threat model and component layer should be the same graph. What is defensible is the record and the loop, not the model; the scripts are Apache-2.0 and a founder needs a second language and a first customer, not permission.

Decided by. Nobody yet: a candidate for Dinis to read, comment on and decide (keep, rewrite, merge, cut, article first, later, not doing it).

Note. One of the business plans published for founders on sgit.ai; an example of the book's advice to start a company from a published plan.
